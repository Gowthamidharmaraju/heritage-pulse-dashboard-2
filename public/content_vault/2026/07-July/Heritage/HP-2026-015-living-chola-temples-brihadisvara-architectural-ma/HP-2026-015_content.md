# Living Chola Temples: Brihadisvara Architectural Marvels

Built by Raja Raja Chola I between 1003 and 1010 CE, the Brihadisvara Temple at Thanjavur represents the zenith of Dravidian temple architecture.

## The Granite Marvel

The entire superstructure rises over 66 meters, topped by an 80-tonne monolithic cupola that was raised via a six-kilometer inclined earth ramp.
